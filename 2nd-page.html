Garlic noodles

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Your favorite noodles
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the choice of noodles until fully cooked.
  2. add minced garlic into a warm pan and saute.
  3. add the cooked noodles into the pan and add as much salt and pepper your heart desires.
  4. plate noodles on a plate. Serve hot.
